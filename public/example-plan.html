The rule you live by
“Payday first: the fixed things get paid the moment money lands, before anything else is bought.”
What you pay first is what gets paid. Everything after that is what is left, and you can see it.
Costs you: The first Friday feels poorer, because the money that used to be spent by Tuesday is gone on Friday.

FOCUS 1
Get out of the mashonisa's book
 Steps 1–3
You said R200 a week goes to the mashonisa on R800 owed.
1
Know what a mashonisa can and cannot do to youNational Credit Regulator complaints line
Read the rules once, and save the NCR consumer line in your phone before the next payment is due.
Setting up National Credit Regulator complaints line
1.Write to the lender or counsellor first: what is wrong, what you want, and a date; the NCR asks whether you tried, and the letter becomes evidence.
2.Gather the papers: the credit agreement, statements, proof of payments, and every SMS or WhatsApp; take photos with your phone if you have only paper.
3.Phone the NCR on 0860 627 627 (Monday to Thursday 8am to 5pm, Friday to 4:30pm) to check a lender's or counsellor's registration and to ask how to lodge; or email complaints@ncr.org.za.
4.Download the complaint initiation form (form 29) from ncr.org.za, fill it in with plain facts and dates, and send it with the papers; if someone lodges for you, they need your signed consent form too.
5.For an unregistered lender (mashonisa) say so clearly: no NCRCP number, the amount lent, what you have paid, and whether they hold your card or ID; the NCR investigates unregistered lending.
6.Write the reference number down and follow up by phone after 20 business days if you have heard nothing.
7.Never pay anyone to lodge a complaint for you, and be careful of emails or SMSes claiming to be from the NCR; the NCR has warned of a cyber-attack on its systems, so confirm contact details on ncr.org.za.
Done when: you have read the rules and saved the number
 
2
Set the pay-down, and stop the re-borrowingYour phone's notes app as a money log
Pay the R200 on Friday, the day the cash lands, and put the receipt in one place.
How to do it
•Choose the amount you can pay every time without borrowing to do it.
•Choose the day, straight after the money lands.
•Pay it in a way that leaves a record, and ask for the balance each time.
•If they offer a new loan, say no and leave.
•Write down each payment and what is left.
Setting up Your phone's notes app as a money log
1.Open the notes app that is already on your phone (Samsung Notes, Google Keep, Apple Notes) and make a page called 'Week 1' with today's date.
2.Each time money leaves your hands, write one line the same day: 'taxi R18', 'airtime R12', 'bread R19'; include cash, the small things and money given to a child.
3.On day 7, add it up, write the total at the bottom, and circle the 3 biggest lines; those are where your month plan starts.
4.Make a second page called 'Owed': one line per debt with who, how much is left, what you pay a month and the interest or fee; put the most expensive at the top.
5.Make a third page called 'Policies': each funeral policy or society with the premium, the payout, who is covered and the waiting period.
6.Make a fourth called 'Waiting' for anything unplanned you want to buy; it waits there 24 hours before any money moves.
7.Pin the pages or put them in a folder called 'Money', and never store a PIN, password or card number in a note; the notes app is not a safe.
Done when: two payments have gone on the day you said
 
3
Get your card and ID back, and keep themOpen a case at SAPS
Ask for the card back on the day the last payment is made, and change the PIN that day.
Setting up Open a case at SAPS
1.Go to the nearest police station; any station must open the case even if the crime happened in another area, and for an emergency phone 10111.
2.Tell the officer at the desk you want to open a case and give a statement; bring your ID, any messages or screenshots, and for a stolen card the bank's fraud reference.
3.Give the facts in order with dates and names; the officer writes it as a statement you sign, so read it before you sign.
4.Ask for the CAS (case) number before you leave and write it down; without it there is no case.
5.Ask for the name of the investigating officer and the station's number so you can follow up the next working day.
6.If the desk refuses to open a case, ask calmly for the shift commander, or phone the SAPS National Complaints line on 0800 333 177; nobody may be refused a case.
7.Give the CAS number to your bank, SAFPS and the NCR; and never pay anyone at the station or anyone who offers to 'make the docket move'.
Done when: everything is back in your hands and the PIN is changed

 Steps 4–5

How to do it

Your phone's notes app as a money log: set it up as shown in step 2.
Done when: seven days are written down
 
5
Split the money into three pots on paydayA Google Sheets month plan
Fixed things, food and transport, and what is left, in that order, the day the money lands.
How to do it
•On payday, before anything is spent, write what came in at the top of a page.
•Under it, list the bills and debts that must be paid, with the day each is due.
•Take those off the top. What is left is for the month.
•Decide what goes aside, and what is for food, transport and the rest.
•Check the plan against the real spending after two weeks and fix what did not fit.
Setting up A Google Sheets month plan
1.Install Google Sheets from the official app store (free with a Google account) and create a new sheet called 'Month plan'.
2.In row 1 type the money that lands on payday (after deductions, lowest month if it changes); in the rows below make three blocks with a heading each: 'Bills' (rent, electricity, every debt minimum, each with its due day), 'Aside' (cushion transfer, family amount), and 'The rest'.
3.Under each block type the amounts in one column, and at the bottom of each block put a sum formula: =SUM() over that block's amounts.
4.At the bottom put the calculation that matters: money in, minus Bills, minus Aside; what is left is 'The rest', and divide it by the weeks to the next payday for a weekly food-and-transport number.
5.On payday, open the sheet, type what actually landed, and pay Bills and move Aside the same day; if the bottom number is negative, that is a debt problem, and the owed list comes first.
6.After 2 weeks, compare the sheet with your notes-app log and fix the lines that did not fit.
7.Never keep account numbers, PINs or passwords in the sheet; amounts and names only.
Done when: the three amounts are written for next month
 
FOCUS 3
Build a cushion you can reach
 Steps 6–7
Once the R200 a week stops going out, it needs somewhere to go.
6
Open the savings pocket and name itGoTyme GoalSave
Open a savings pocket in the banking app and name it for Christmas, so the money has a job.
How to do it
•Think of the last three surprises that cost you money, and write what each one cost.
•Pick the smallest of them, or one week of your essentials, as the first target.
•Write the number somewhere you will see it on payday.
•When you reach it, pick the next number.
Setting up GoTyme GoalSave
1.Open the GoTyme Bank app, tap 'Save' and choose 'GoalSave'; opening one is free and you can have several (GoTyme's page says up to 20, with a combined cap of R250,000).
2.Name it for its job, 'Cushion' or 'School fees', and type the target number from your plan; the name is what stops you spending it.
3.Read the interest terms on the screen at that moment: GoTyme's GoalSave page describes 6% a year from day one and up to 10% a year if you give 10 days' notice before closing, while other published descriptions differ, so the app is the source.
4.Move the first amount today from your EveryDay account, even R20, so the pocket exists before payday.
5.Set a recurring transfer to the GoalSave for the day after your money lands, for the amount in pot 2 of your month plan.
6.When you need the money, choose the notice option if you can wait 10 days to earn the full interest; immediate withdrawal is allowed but may pay less.
7.Never borrow to put money into a GoalSave; no savings rate beats the interest on a loan.
Done when: the pocket exists with a name
